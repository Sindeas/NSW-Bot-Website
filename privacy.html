Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the New Shinobi Warriors Discord Bot ("Bot") may process when you use it.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the Bot's features, the Bot may process basic Discord information such as your Discord user ID, username or display name, server ID, channel ID, and information you voluntarily provide through Bot commands.

2. Game and Bot Data

If the Bot provides New Shinobi Warriors game-related features, it may store information necessary to provide those features, such as character names, game-related identifiers, settings, levels, or other information you intentionally submit.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the Bot, provide requested features, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve the New Shinobi Warriors community experience.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate the Bot.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or protect the Bot, its users, or the New Shinobi Warriors project.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation, security, recordkeeping, or the feature for which it was collected.

6. Data Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your Discord account, or want to request deletion of data controlled by the NSW Bot, contact the New Shinobi Warriors project through its official Discord support/community channels.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot does not bypass Discord's age requirements or applicable children's privacy laws. Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable law.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the official New Shinobi Warriors Discord support/community channels.
